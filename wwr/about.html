Diani White Water Rafting

At Diani White Water Rafting, adventure is more than a trip—it’s an experience. We combine thrilling rapids, breathtaking scenery, professional guides, and a strong commitment to safety to create unforgettable moments on the water. Come paddle, explore, laugh, and discover the wild side of Kenya with us.

History

Diani White Water Rafting was born from a passion for adventure, nature, and the thrill of exploring Kenya’s waterways. Inspired by the growing adventure-tourism scene along the Kenyan coast, we set out to create memorable rafting experiences that combine excitement, teamwork, and breathtaking scenery. From our beginnings as a small adventure-focused company, we have grown with a commitment to safety, excellent customer service, and responsible tourism. Today, we welcome both local and international visitors looking to experience the excitement of white-water rafting while discovering another side of Kenya’s natural beauty.
